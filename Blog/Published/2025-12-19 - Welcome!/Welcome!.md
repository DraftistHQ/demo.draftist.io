You've just discovered the **Demo Site** for [Draftist](https://draftist.io), a publishing platform that transforms your [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) notes into beautiful, fast websites. ^c71c42

This site showcases what's possible when you publish with Draftist. Every post here demonstrates different content features - from rich text formatting to image galleries, code blocks, and more. The goal isn't to teach you *how* to use these features (that's what the documentation is for), but to show you what your published content can look like. ^c509e3

> [!info] About This Demo
> This is a living example of Draftist's capabilities. Each post is written as if it were real blog content, but carefully crafted to naturally demonstrate specific features. Think of it as "show, don't tell" for what your site could become.
^5474fa

## Why Draftist? ^320c29

Draftist bridges the gap between your private knowledge base and public publishing. Write in Obsidian with all its power and flexibility, then publish to a fast, beautiful site with a single click. ^9d5243

**Key benefits:** ^94f096
- **Write in Obsidian**
  Use the editor you love with all your favorite plugins. ^bb5081
- **Beautifully optimized**
  Content and images are fine-tuned for speed and visual appeal. ^4fee47
- **Rich content support**
  Code blocks, galleries, callouts, embedded videos, and more. ^40b254
- **Custom domains**
  Publish to your own domain with automatic SSL. ^6e58e9
